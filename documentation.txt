Overview
Visteon Meetings integrates with Zoom and allows users to authenticate using QR code login, connect their Zoom account and start or join meetings.

Adding the app
Prerequisites
* Active Zoom account
* Access to Visteon Meetings Automotive Android App
* Device capable of scanning QR codes or any device with a web browser to manually enter the code

Steps
1. Open Visteon Meetings.
2. Scan the QR code displayed on the login screen or open the link displayed on the screen in your browser and enter the code and click 'Confirm'.
3. Review the scopes/permissions that the app requires and click 'Approve'.
4. Return to Visteon Meetings after authorization.

Using Visteon Meetings
Start/Join scheduled Meeting
1. Open Visteon Meetings.
2. Select the home tab.
3. Click Start/Join Meeting on the Scheduled Meeting List.

Removing the app
1. Sign in to Zoom Marketplace.
2. Navigate to Manage -> Added Apps.
3. Select Visteon Meetings.
4. Click Remove.
5. Confirm removal.

After Removal
- Zoom access tokens are revoked.
- Zoom integration access is removed.
- Reauthorization is required to reconnect.
